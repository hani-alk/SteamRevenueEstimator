What's baked in

Steam's cut is tiered. 30% of gross up to $10M lifetime, 25% from $10M to $50M, 20% above that. Most games never leave the first tier.

Gross is already net of VAT and sales tax — that's how Valve reports it, so there's no extra line for it here.

Refunds are removed as units before revenue, so they cost you the full sale rather than a percentage of it.

The $100 Steam Direct fee, storefront localisation, ports, and your own costs are not modelled. This is revenue, not profit.
